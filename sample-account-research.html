Fictional worked examples · Company-level research

Industrial account-research sample and template

Fictional throughout. Every company, source statement and decision below is invented for demonstration. These are not researched businesses, client results, available projects or records approved for delivery. There are no personal contacts.

The examples show how a written profile changes an inclusion decision—and what an additional project signal does not establish. They are teaching examples, not a sample batch or a demonstration of the 90/10 target proportions.

01 / Example brief

The fictional targeting brief

Example profile DEMO-PROFILE-01: an industrial process-equipment supplier wants to research food-and-beverage manufacturing companies operating production facilities in Ireland. The record unit is the company. Existing accounts and known channel conflicts would be excluded in a real brief; this invented exercise contains none.

Exclude residential-only retailers and businesses without relevant manufacturing activity. An active project is not required for the industry-matched class. For this exercise, the opportunity-signal class requires a source statement identifying a proposed facility expansion at a relevant manufacturing company.

This brief tests account relevance only. It does not assess installed equipment, hygienic requirements, technical compatibility, purchasing authority or buying timing.

02 / Account fit

Accepted for account fit: Fictional Beverage Manufacturer A

Fictional example · DEMO-ACCOUNT-A · Accepted within this illustration only.

Company and territory
Fictional Beverage Manufacturer A; beverage manufacturing; Ireland; company-level record.
Invented source statement
DEMO-SOURCE-A: an invented company activity statement says the business operates a beverage-production facility in Ireland. No live source was retrieved.
Fit reason
The stipulated activity and location meet the example profile’s manufacturing and territory rules.
Research class
Industry-matched lead (Look Alike): fit_matched_lookalike.
Project research status
Not assessed—not required: not_assessed_not_required. This is not a finding that no project exists.
Unknowns
Installed equipment, specifications, current needs and purchasing route are not established.
Next check
In real work, verify company identity and source-supported fit, assess source-use rights and complete duplicate/conflict checks before an acceptance decision.

Why include it? The written account criteria are met in the fictional scenario. No project or buyer-intent claim is needed to explain that fit.

03 / Project signal

Paired signal example: Fictional Beverage Manufacturer B

Fictional example · DEMO-SIGNAL-B · Accepted within this illustration only.

Company and territory
Fictional Beverage Manufacturer B; beverage manufacturing; Ireland; company-level record.
Invented source statements
DEMO-SOURCE-B-FIT describes an Irish beverage-production business. DEMO-SOURCE-B-PROJECT says it proposes adding production space at its facility. Both statements are invented; no planning record or company announcement was retrieved.
Observed fact within the fiction
A proposed facility expansion is described. Construction, procurement and equipment selection are not established.
Fit and signal decision
The company meets the same account profile, and the stipulated expansion meets this example’s signal definition.
Research class and status
Opportunity-signal lead: opportunity_signal; qualifying signal identified: qualifying_signal_identified—within the invented scenario only.
Interpretation
The proposed change gives a reason to investigate relevance to the supplier’s range. It does not establish a requirement for a particular product.
Unknowns and next check
Confirm actual stage, specifications and procurement route in real work. Package availability, buyer access and intent remain unknown. No outreach is authorised by the example.

What changed? There is an additional project-evidence requirement, not a higher guarantee of quality or conversion. A real signal record would also need dates, current evidence, rights checks and independent QA.

04 / Rejection

Rejected for fit: Fictional Home Pump Retailer C

Fictional example · DEMO-REJECT-C · Rejected within this illustration.

Company and territory
Fictional Home Pump Retailer C; residential equipment retail; Ireland; company-level candidate.
Invented source statement
DEMO-SOURCE-C says the business sells domestic pumps to householders and does not conduct food-or-beverage manufacturing. This is an invented positive description, not an inference from missing information.
Tested class
Candidate assessed for fit_matched_lookalike; not accepted or counted as a delivered lead.
Project research status
not_assessed_not_required.
Failed criterion
DEMO-PROFILE-01 requires relevant manufacturing activity and excludes residential-only retailers.
Decision reason
Business-type mismatch. A shared product word—“pump”—does not override the targeting brief.
Next step
Exclude from this example scope. Do not invent a project or soften the criterion to make the candidate fit.

This is a rejection against one brief, not a claim that the business would be unsuitable for every supplier.

05 / Downloads

Edit the fields without treating the sample as live data.

The blank worksheet and small field dictionary are available without registration. They are an educational starting point for discussing company-level research, not the full internal QA schema or an agreed delivery specification.

  • Blank account worksheet (CSV)
  • Editable field dictionary (CSV)
  • Fictional worked rows (CSV)

The fields keep identity, profile, source references, dates, class, project status, interpretation, unknowns and decision separate. Dates are blank in the fictional rows because no source was published or reviewed. Demonstration source IDs are not live citations.

A real record needs permitted sources, current identity and fit evidence, applicable data controls and the agreed review/release checks. Downloading or completing a worksheet does not clear source rights or authorise outreach.

To check more than one record, use the worked batch audit and acceptance worksheets to reconcile company counts, duplicates, holds and rejections.
