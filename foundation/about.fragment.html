About Demand Range

Industrial prospects. Matched to your market.

Demand Range is an industrial lead research and market intelligence business. We help suppliers identify prospects that match their target industries and agreed customer profile.

Our model combines a broad base of industry-matched Look Alike leads with a smaller share supported by identified project or development signals. Relevance comes first; an active project is not required for every lead.

What guides the work.

  • Industry fit first. Work from written target criteria and explicit exclusions.
  • Evidence where it matters. Keep source-supported facts separate from interpretation.
  • Visible limits. Record unknowns instead of implying a ready buyer or accessible package.
  • Agreed scope. Define the record unit, fields and commercial terms before work begins.

An Irish business serving Ireland, the UK and the EU.

Demand Range is an Irish business serving suppliers selling into Ireland, the UK and the EU. Each country is scoped on its own sources and language needs, and other territory preferences can be submitted for assessment; they are not a promise of worldwide coverage.

The service described here is company-level research. Named-person delivery and outreach are separate, unagreed scopes unless expressly authorised and confirmed. The site does not offer a live research platform or guarantee sales outcomes.
