## Rights posture

- **New copy:** original wording informed by the user's internal brand/product/plan sources. No third-party article excerpts, database exports, scraped contact data, stock imagery or client material were added.
- **Fictional sources:** DEMO-SOURCE identifiers refer only to invented source statements printed in the page/CSV. They are not fake live citations or retrieval receipts. Publication/review dates remain blank because no real sources were retrieved.
- **Real data remains pending:** source accessibility, citation or paraphrase does not establish customer-use rights. No provider contract, permission or legal basis is declared cleared by this task.
- **Legacy sample:** its existing fictional company, data and PDF stay fictional. Linking it does not update the PDF, approve technical suitability or make its illustrative score/time window a service promise.
- **Educational CSVs:** no contact/domain fields or live record data. No licence/redistribution warranty or new legal promise is added. Public release and final download presentation belong to the parent review.
- **Visuals:** no logos, fonts, images or binary assets created or changed. Existing OG artwork requires a separate visual check.
