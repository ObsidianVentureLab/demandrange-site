Full data table

Every row of the CSV, for all 31 activities: mining (B), manufacturing (C and each division C10–C33), energy supply (D35) and water, sewerage and waste (E36–E39).[1][2] Pick a country or a sector from the index to jump to its rows. The EU-27 aggregate comes first, then the 27 member states in Eurostat’s order. On a phone, scroll each table sideways.

Columns, in order: enterprises total; 10–19; 20–49; 50–249; 250+ (enterprises by persons employed); persons employed; net turnover (EUR m); machinery and equipment investment (EUR m); Eurostat flags.

A blank cell means Eurostat withheld (C, confidential) or didn’t publish the value. “e” means Eurostat estimate. The flags column names each flagged column, for example “250+: C”.

How many food manufacturers with 50+ staff are there in Ireland? Go to Ireland → C10: 147 + 56 = 203.

847 of the 868 rows have an enterprise total and 776 have a machinery-investment value. 505 cells are flagged C and 131 are flagged e. The values here are the same as in the CSV download; the page adds thousands separators and a typographic minus for reading.
