What to include in your enquiry

  • What you sell: your product or service range
  • Target sectors or industries
  • Countries, within Ireland, the UK and the EU
  • The type of account list: companies, operating sites or distributors
  • Approximate volume: roughly how many accounts
  • Your deadline
  • How the list will be used, for example territory planning or account prioritisation
  • Exclusions, described in general terms (for example existing customers or a type of company), not as a list

Do not send passwords, credentials, sensitive personal data, suppression lists, confidential client files or live campaign data in a first email.

We reply within 2 working days.
