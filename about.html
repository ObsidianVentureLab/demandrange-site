About Demand Range

Building the market-intelligence layer industrial sales teams have been missing.

The harder question is not “who could buy?” It is “what is changing, why might it matter and when should we engage?”

The observation

Account data is not market timing.

Industrial sellers can spend hours finding accounts and contacts while the evidence of change remains fragmented across public records, company activity and the project ecosystem.

Demand Range starts by connecting that evidence into analyst-led opportunity records. Repeatable software is a later stage—only after paid use, trust and sufficient market coverage justify it.

Build sequence

Trust before automation.

The current offer is a service. The longer-term direction is shaped by what real customer use proves useful.

Now

Analyst-led intelligence

Defined markets, sourced evidence, human qualification and practical briefs.

Next

Repeatable software

Only where recurring work and customer feedback support a trustworthy workflow.

Later

Broader market layer

Category and geographic expansion when the playbook and evidence coverage are ready.

Starting point

Ireland first. One market at a time.

Each engagement is a focused proving ground: one product category, one territory, across food, beverage, pharma, chemical, water and manufacturing facilities. Coverage depends on an agreed category playbook.

Show the evidence

Make the supporting record accessible and separate it from interpretation.

Make uncertainty visible

Unknowns and review dates matter as much as a prioritisation score.

Protect customer trust

Use outcomes only where the client shares them; never present confidential information as market evidence.

Measure predictions

Review the thesis against outcomes rather than rewarding research volume.

Let use shape software

Build future tooling from proven workflows, not from a premature platform claim.

Build durable memory

Create a market asset that improves through governed feedback—not an activity factory.

Bring a market worth understanding better.

We will tell you whether the current analyst-led approach is a fit.
