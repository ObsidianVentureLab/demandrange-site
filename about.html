About Demand Range

About Demand Range: industrial account research for Ireland, the UK and the EU

Demand Range is an Irish industrial account research business serving Ireland, the UK and the EU. We build evidence-backed target account lists for suppliers of industrial equipment, technology and services. Each account is matched to your written profile, and each record shows why the company fits and what still needs checking.

01 / what we do

What we do

We research companies, and the sites they operate, that match a supplier's target market. A typical brief names the industries, the territory, the size of company, the site activity that matters and the accounts to exclude. We then find the companies that fit, check them against public evidence and deliver a list with the reason each one is included.

Most accounts are industry-matched: they fit the profile, whether or not they have a current project. A smaller share also carry evidence of a relevant project or development, such as a planning permission, a licence application or an announced expansion. Relevance comes first; an active project is not required for every account. Opportunity Intelligence is the specialist service for research that does need project context.

This is company-level account research; named contacts and outreach are separate scopes, not included by default.

We call this work industrial account research: finding the companies and sites that fit a written brief and recording the public evidence for each.

02 / who runs the research

Who runs the research

Each project is run by a research analyst working from the written brief. The analyst agrees the profile and exclusions with you before any research starts, finds and checks the candidate companies, and records the evidence for each one. The analyst who builds a list also writes down what could not be established, so you can see the limits of each record rather than assume them.

A score never replaces the fit decision. A company is included because it meets the written criteria, and the record says which source supports each one.

03 / sectors we research

Sectors we research

We work with suppliers selling into process and manufacturing industries, including:

  • water and wastewater;
  • pharmaceuticals and life sciences;
  • food and beverage;
  • chemicals;
  • other manufacturing and industrial sites where a public register or filing shows what the site does.

Each country in Ireland, the UK and the EU is scoped on its own sources and language needs, and coverage is agreed in writing for each project, not assumed.

04 / how we check the research

How we check the research

Every list goes through the same checks before it is released:

  • Fit: each account meets the written criteria, and each material criterion has a source reference and a date.
  • Identity: the record names the right company and site, with parent and subsidiary relationships and duplicates resolved, so that several sites are not counted as several companies.
  • Evidence: facts from sources are kept separate from our interpretation, and old sources are not presented as current.
  • Exclusions: accounts you asked us to leave out, such as existing customers, are removed.
  • Claims: nothing in the record implies buying intent, an open tender or a ready buyer unless the evidence supports it.

Our research method explains these checks in more detail, and the fictional account-record sample shows what a finished record looks like.

05 / how we work with data

How we work with data

We research from public sources: company registers, environmental and regulatory registers, planning records, official statistics and companies' own published information. Public access does not by itself give a right to reuse, so we check how each source may be used before its content goes into a deliverable.

Our research is company-level by default. We do not collect or deliver personal data about named individuals unless that is separately agreed in writing, with a lawful basis under the GDPR and the controls that go with it. We do not ask you to send us customer lists or personal data in an enquiry. Our privacy notice explains how we handle the details you send us.

06 / frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What does Demand Range deliver?

A list of companies, or of sites, that match your written profile, with the reason each one is included, the public sources that support it and what is still unknown.

Does every account have an active project?

No. Most accounts are matched on industry fit. A smaller share carry evidence of a relevant project or development, and the record says which is which.

Do you provide contact names or run outreach?

Not by default. Company-level account research is the standard service. Named contacts and outreach are separate scopes that need their own written agreement.

Which countries do you cover?

Ireland, the UK and the EU. Each country is scoped on its own. Anything outside Ireland, the UK and the EU is outside standard coverage.
