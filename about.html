About Demand Range

Industrial prospects. Matched to your market.

Demand Range is an Irish industrial lead and account research business serving Ireland, the UK and the EU. We build analyst-checked, industry-matched target account lists for suppliers of industrial equipment, technology and services, covering sectors such as water, pharma, food and beverage, and chemicals. Where public evidence exists, each account comes with project or development context.

Our model combines a broad base of industry-matched Look Alike leads with a smaller share supported by identified project or development signals. Relevance comes first; an active project is not required for every lead.

01 / purpose

Make the inclusion reason clear.

The purpose is practical: help industrial suppliers focus their prospecting on relevant businesses. A useful account record should explain why the company fits, what supports that view and what still needs checking.

We call this work industrial account research: finding the companies and sites that fit a written brief and recording the public evidence for each.

Opportunity Intelligence remains a specialist capability for research that needs project or development context. It is part of the wider offer, not a requirement that every account has a project.

Explore Opportunity Intelligence

02 / priorities

What guides the work.

  • Industry fit first. Work from written target criteria and explicit exclusions.
  • Evidence where it matters. Keep source-supported facts separate from interpretation.
  • Visible limits. Record unknowns instead of implying a ready buyer or accessible package.
  • Agreed scope. Define the record unit, fields and commercial terms before work begins.

03 / focus

An Irish business serving Ireland, the UK and the EU.

Each country is scoped on its own sources and language needs, and other territory preferences can be submitted for assessment; they are not a promise of worldwide coverage.

The service described here is company-level research. Named-person delivery and outreach are separate, unagreed scopes unless expressly authorised and confirmed. The site does not offer a live research platform or guarantee sales outcomes.

04 / Next step
