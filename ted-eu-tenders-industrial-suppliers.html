TED EU tenders for industrial suppliers: search and triage guide

By Demand Range research team · Last reviewed 28 October 2026

TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) is the website of the Supplement to the Official Journal of the EU, where public buyers publish procurement notices above the EU thresholds. Filter by place of performance and notice type, read prior information notices as early signals and award notices as a route to the winning contractor, and check whether the buyer is a utility.

TED is the EU above-threshold layer, not the whole market. Most below-threshold contracts stay on national portals such as Ireland's eTenders, and UK notices left TED for Find a Tender on 31 December 2020.

02 / what ted covers and what it does not

What TED covers, and what it does not

Public buyers publish notices in the Supplement to the Official Journal of the EU on the TED website, using the eForms standard, which became mandatory in October 2023.[1] EU rules apply in full to tenders above set values that are presumed to be of cross-border interest; for lower-value tenders national rules apply.[2]

So TED will not show you:

  • Below-threshold contracts, which are advertised nationally, for example on Ireland's eTenders.
  • UK procurement. Find a Tender replaced TED in the UK on 31 December 2020. Since 24 February 2025 it carries both above- and below-threshold notices for new UK procurements, except below-threshold notices in Scotland.[3]
  • Private-sector projects, such as a manufacturer's own plant expansion. Those need project research, not a tenders portal.
03 / the eu thresholds for 2026 and 2027

The EU thresholds for 2026 and 2027

From 1 January 2026, the EU thresholds (excluding value added tax) are:[2][4][5]

Works contracts, all directives
€5,404,000
Supplies and services, central government authorities
€140,000
Supplies and services, sub-central contracting authorities
€216,000
Supplies and services, utilities (water, energy, transport, postal)
€432,000

For an equipment supplier this matters in two ways. A €300,000 pump or instrument package is below the €432,000 utilities threshold if a water utility buys it, so it may never reach TED, but above the €140,000 threshold if a central government authority buys it. And a large works contract may contain your equipment without naming it, so the opportunity is with the contractor, not the buyer.

04 / set up a ted search you can repeat

Set up a TED search you can repeat

TED's advanced search filters by CPV code, nature of contract, place of performance (the main location of the works or delivery), value, procedure, buyer, legal basis and dates.[7] CPV codes come from the Common Procurement Vocabulary set by Regulation (EC) No 213/2008.[6] Choose your codes first: our CPV codes for pumps, valves and equipment page lists the codes, their parents and how alert matching works.

  • Pick the right scope. "Active notices" means planning notices from the past 364 days, competitions still before their deadline and results from the past 99 days. "All notices" goes back ten years.[7]
  • Tick "Include only the latest versions" so a changed notice does not appear twice.[7]
  • Add place of performance for your countries. Buyers write in their own language, so a code-and-place search catches notices an English keyword search misses. Run a keyword search alongside as a check, because buyers choose codes imperfectly.
  • Save it as an alert. With an EU Login account you can keep up to 25 saved searches and 25 search alerts, sent daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, or follow them as RSS feeds. TED deletes accounts after 120 days without use.[9]
05 / read the notice type plan compete award

Read the notice type: plan, compete, award

Planning: prior information notices
Published before a call for competition, these are your earliest signal. Some are for information only; some shorten tender deadlines; some act as the call for competition itself.[1] Record the buyer, scope and expected timing, and diary a check.
Competition: contract notices
The live tender. Decide quickly whether you would bid as prime, partner with a bidder or supply the eventual winner.
Result: contract award notices
Identify who won. For equipment suppliers this is often the most useful notice: the winning contractor may now be buying the packages you sell. One competition can lead to several result notices when lots are awarded at different times.[1] Our EPC and EPCM guide covers that route.

A change notice, such as a deadline extension, gets its own publication number. The changes are listed in section 10, and the earlier versions stay on TED, so always record which version you read.[10]

06 / check whether the buyer is a utility

Check whether the buyer is a utility

Directive 2014/24/EU covers procurement by public authorities generally. Directive 2014/25/EU covers entities operating in water, energy, transport and postal services, with its own €432,000 threshold for supplies and services.[2] For pumps, valves, instrumentation and treatment equipment, utilities are frequently the buyer, so check which regime a notice uses before comparing values. The Irish water investment guide shows the same question for one national utility.

07 / five real ted notices read the fields not just the match

Five real TED notices: read the fields, not just the match

We read these five notices on TED on 28 October 2026. They are dated examples of what to check, not an open-opportunity list; two deadlines have already passed. Verify the latest version and the procurement documents before acting on any notice.

Notice What the fields show Question to answer before triage
683590-2026, Netherlands, published 5 October 2026[11] Main CPV 42122000 Pumps, nine lots. Lot 1 supplies sewage submersible pumps; lot 5 (Perceel 5A) describes repair and overhaul, yet its main-nature field says Supplies. Tender deadline 20 November 2026, 11:00 UTC. Does the lot buy equipment, a repair outcome, or both, and who supplies the parts?
706732-2026, Malta, published 13 October 2026[12] Lot 1 "Valves" and lot 2 "Couplings and Flange Adaptors" repeat the same supply-and-delivery description for a trunk-mains upgrade. Deadline 11 November 2026, 09:30 (UTC+02:00). Which line items belong to which lot? Only the schedule answers that.
713010-2026, Portugal, published 15 October 2026[13] CPV 42124000 Parts of pumps, compressors, engines or motors. The procedure's estimated value is 0 EUR, lot 1 is estimated at 10,000 EUR and shows a 182,500 EUR framework maximum. What does each value cover? Record each figure with its label rather than adding them up.
644250-2026, Denmark, published 18 September 2026[14] A change notice extending the deadline for monoflange valves to 25 September 2026. The lot title, the description and the place-of-performance field give different delivery locations. Where does delivery actually go? Check the price schedule and delivery terms before pricing freight.
208240-2026, Germany, published 26 March 2026[15] A works lot for a pumping station, with 42122000 Pumps as an additional code. The selection criteria ask for three comparable projects in ten years, such as pipework of DN500 or more or a pumping station of at least 500 m³/h. Is there a separate equipment scope? A reference-project threshold is not the pump duty.
08 / triage each notice by your role

Triage each notice by your role

For every notice, record one decision: bid, partner or supply the winner, watch or not relevant, with the reason. The free tender triage worksheet (CSV) keeps the notice reference, scope, your role and next check together. The eTenders guide works through fictional triage cases for Irish notices.

Fictional example: a valve maker finds a prior information notice from a regional water utility for treatment-works upgrades in two years. It is not a bid for the valve maker, but it is a watch: diary the expected contract notice and, later, the award notice that names the contractor.

To monitor at scale, TED's Search API can be used anonymously for expert queries and bulk downloads.[8]

09 / frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What is TED?
TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) is the online version of the Supplement to the Official Journal of the European Union, where public buyers in the EU publish procurement notices, mainly for contracts above the EU thresholds.
Is TED free to use?
Yes. TED notices can be searched on the website, and the TED Search API does not require authentication. Saved searches and alerts need a free EU Login account.
What are the EU procurement thresholds for 2026?
From 1 January 2026 the EU thresholds are €5,404,000 for works, €140,000 for supplies and services bought by central government, €216,000 for sub-central authorities, and €432,000 for supplies and services bought by utilities. They apply for 2026 and 2027.
Are UK tenders on TED?
No. Find a Tender replaced TED for UK notices on 31 December 2020. Since 24 February 2025 new UK procurements under the Procurement Act 2023 are published on Find a Tender; see our UK public tenders guide.
Which CPV codes should I search on TED?
Use the codes for what you sell plus the works and maintenance codes buyers bundle them into. Our CPV codes guide lists them with parent codes and a free CSV.
What is a prior information notice?
A prior information notice is a planning notice that a buyer publishes before a call for competition. It is the earliest public signal of an upcoming tender on TED.
Should I only look at tenders I can bid for directly?
No. Contract award notices name the winning contractor, who may then buy equipment and services from suppliers. That route is often more relevant to equipment makers than bidding as prime.
