Irish industrial distributor research: building a channel shortlist

A company selling relevant equipment is not automatically a suitable distributor for your range. Before building a shortlist, decide what the prospective channel would need to do: resell components, hold stock, support installation, service equipment, or develop a particular application market. Those are different research questions.

This guide proposes a company-level research method for industrial suppliers considering an Irish distribution route. It is not a directory of available partners. The worked cases are wholly fictional, and no company is represented as willing or able to take on a new range.

01 / start with the channel job not a broad company label

Start with the channel job, not a broad company label

Write a short brief that another researcher could apply without asking you what “a good distributor” means:

  • Product boundary: the component or equipment family being considered. Avoid treating all pumps, valves or controls as interchangeable.
  • Application boundary: the uses and customer environments you want to investigate. Sector marketing is not evidence of technical compatibility.
  • Territory rule: whether you require an Irish operating location, service coverage in Ireland, or another expressly defined relationship. Those are separate conditions.
  • Channel job: for example, resale and stockholding, or resale with a separately evidenced repair capability. Record which requirements are mandatory.
  • Exclusions: existing partners, client-supplied protected accounts, retail-only operations, or unsuitable application categories. Apply only the exclusions actually agreed.
  • Unknowns to preserve: appetite for a new principal, competing agreements, financial capacity, commercial terms and technical suitability unless separately established.

Do not attach an active-project requirement unless the brief needs one. A company can match an account-research brief without a current purchasing event.

02 / test the product application and territory separately

Test the product, application and territory separately

Use a candidate's own product and service information to formulate a narrow inclusion reason. Where available, compare it with a manufacturer's current distributor information. Record what each source actually establishes, its date when stated, and when it was checked. Respect applicable source-use conditions; this method is not permission to copy directories or republish their records.

A reference to a product family does not establish coverage of every model, material, pressure range or application. A service described on a group website does not automatically establish that an Irish branch performs it. If a source does not answer your precise question, leave the answer unresolved rather than widening the claim.

For a candidate serving several markets, retain the activity relevant to the brief. Do not count the same business again because it also installs or repairs equipment. Record additional roles against the same resolved company identity.

03 / separate an existing relationship from an available partnership

Separate an existing relationship from an available partnership

A publicly described relationship with a manufacturer is evidence to investigate, not a verdict about your own route to market. Keep these questions separate:

  1. Which relationship is actually described, for which product range and territory?
  2. How current is the source, and does another source disagree?
  3. Is any exclusivity or restriction expressly evidenced for the relevant scope?
  4. Is there evidence of willingness to discuss your range? If not, leave willingness unknown.

Do not infer exclusivity from a logo. Conversely, the absence of a competing brand on a website does not prove the company is free to represent you. A contractual assessment is outside this research exercise.

Use a conflict flag to prompt review, not to manufacture a legal conclusion. Where an existing client or supplier relationship creates a handling restriction, apply the agreed restriction before sharing or acting on the shortlist.

04 / three fictional screening decisions

Three fictional screening decisions

The following invented brief seeks businesses with an Irish operation that resell industrial isolation valves and provide documented workshop repair. Stockholding is desirable but not mandatory. Retail-only businesses are excluded. New-partner availability is not assumed.

Fictional Channel A: relevant range, incomplete capability evidence

The invented record describes an Irish operation and resale of industrial isolation valves. It also describes on-site installation, but says nothing about workshop repair.

Decision: hold for the missing repair evidence. Installation is not a substitute for the mandatory capability. Record the range and location evidence without promoting the candidate to a complete match. Stockholding and willingness to discuss a new range remain unknown.

Fictional Channel B: brief match, commercial questions still open

The invented record explicitly describes an Irish operation, the relevant valve resale activity and workshop repair. It also states a relationship with another manufacturer, but supplies no exclusivity wording, stockholding evidence or invitation to new principals.

Decision: retain as a brief-matched research candidate, with the relationship flagged for review. Do not mark it an available partner, approved distributor or exclusive agent. Its commercial suitability remains to be established separately.

Fictional Channel C: relevant product word, wrong route

The invented record describes a retail-only domestic plumbing shop. It uses the word “valves,” but the stipulated activity is domestic retail and contains no industrial isolation-valve resale or workshop-repair evidence.

Decision: exclude under the agreed retail-only rule. The shared product word does not override the route and application exclusions. This is a decision about the invented brief, not the quality of a real business.

05 / produce a shortlist someone can challenge

Produce a shortlist someone can challenge

For each candidate, record the company identity, relevant operation, product/application evidence, mandatory capability evidence, relationship or conflict flag, decision, reason and next question. Keep the source reference beside the fact it supports, rather than attaching one generic homepage link to the whole record.

The accompanying worksheet uses three dispositions: retain_for_review, hold_for_evidence and exclude. “Retain” means the stipulated account criteria are met; it does not mean partnership availability has been verified. Mandatory unknowns trigger a hold. An explicit exclusion triggers an exclusion. Optional unknowns remain visible rather than being quietly scored as present or absent.

Review the shortlist against the original brief before any next step. Remove duplicate company counts, check unresolved scope conflicts, and ask whether every inclusion reason would survive a challenge from the supplier's commercial team. Named-person research and outreach require a separately agreed, lawful process; this guide does not authorise either.

06 / use the blank and fictional worksheets separately

Use the blank and fictional worksheets separately

Open the channel-screening downloads in the resource library. The blank CSV contains headers only; the separate fictional CSV contains the three cases above. Neither file supplies researched companies or personal contacts.

The worksheet is specific to the invented isolation-valve and workshop-repair brief, not a universal distributor score. Before using the blank file for a different brief, rename the product, territory and capability fields to the agreed criteria. Keep mandatory and optional tests separate. One row is one resolved company; preserve additional roles without counting the company again.

Use yes, no or unknown for the capability and retail-only tests. An explicit retail-only exclusion takes precedence; a no on a mandatory criterion excludes; an unknown mandatory criterion holds; only all required yes answers retain for review. Optional stockholding does not change the decision. The file stores your decision; it contains no formulas and performs no automatic validation.

In the blank file, company_name and record_context replace the fictional file's fictional_company and example_status. Keep the fictional records marked wholly_fictional; do not import them as prospects. Each tested fact has an adjacent _evidence_ref field: put the exact source location and supported scope there, not just a generic homepage. An unknown fact can keep its evidence reference blank; record the missing check in the reason. The separate source_reference field is overall record context, not evidence for every cell. Record the date actually checked in checked_date; if individual facts were checked on different dates, include their dates in their respective evidence references rather than implying one review date covers them all. The teaching cases have no external source and no checked date. Keep relationship scope, exclusivity and willingness separate, and use the reason field for the missing evidence or next question.

07 / bring a bounded question to the research brief

Bring a bounded question to the research brief

A useful starting point is: “We want to investigate Irish industrial distributors matching this product, application and support-capability brief, excluding these existing relationships. Separate observed account fit from unknown partnership availability.”

Use the industrial account-research service to discuss the scope. For operator, OEM, integrator or contractor targeting instead, use the pump and flow-control prospecting guide. Review the fictional account sample before requesting a scoped quote.

The method and examples here are original analyst guidance, not a validated scoring system, market-size estimate, technical selection recommendation or evidence of a live commercial opportunity.
