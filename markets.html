High evidence standard

Possibility is not proof.

A facility investment may create demand for your category. It does not prove a package is open, a brand is displaced or procurement has started.

Every brief should answer

  • What change was actually observed?
  • Which applications are plausibly affected?
  • What evidence supports that link?
  • Who may own the specification?
  • Which facts remain unknown?
  • What is the smallest credible next action?
