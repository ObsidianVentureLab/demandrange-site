Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-05-24

This notice explains how Demand Range handles website enquiries. It is a concise website-enquiry notice, not legal advice, and may be updated as services, systems, and legal review mature.

Who we are

Demand Range is an Ireland-based B2B fulfilment partner for demand-generation and content-syndication vendors. Contact: partners@demandrange.com.

Information we collect through this website

If you contact us or request a fulfilment fit check, we may collect the information you provide, such as your name, work email, company, role, campaign region, audience criteria, delivery-template notes, and enquiry details. We may also receive basic technical information normally sent by browsers and hosting systems, such as timestamps, page requests, and security logs.

Do not send live lead files

Please do not submit live lead files, sensitive personal data, passwords, credentials, or confidential client files through the public website or enquiry route. If a campaign fit check progresses, we will agree an appropriate secure route, scope, and review requirements first.

How we use enquiry information

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, assess whether a campaign/spec is a fit, prepare follow-up materials, maintain basic business records, and protect the website and enquiry channel from misuse.

Campaign data is handled separately

Any live campaign fulfilment, lead data processing, data sourcing, telemarketing, suppression handling, or vendor/client delivery work requires separate scope, approved sources, role allocation, privacy/compliance review, and appropriate commercial terms.

Your rights and contact

If you have a privacy question about this website enquiry process, or want to ask about access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, or another data-protection request, contact partners@demandrange.com.
