Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Demand Range handles personal data connected with this public website and email enquiries about Industrial Opportunity Intelligence.

Important: this website has no transmitting form. Please do not send sensitive personal data, passwords, credentials, confidential client files, suppression lists or live campaign data in a first email enquiry.
1. Who we are2. Scope3. Information collected4. How and why we use it5. Lawful bases6. Sharing7. Transfers8. Retention9. Your rights10. Security11. Contact

1. Who we are

Demand Range is a B2B brand providing analyst-led Industrial Opportunity Intelligence for suppliers of industrial equipment, technology and services. Contact partners@demandrange.com.

Confirmed legal-entity details, including registered company name, registered office and company number where applicable, will be added once available. No unverified entity details are published here.

2. Scope

This notice covers the public website at demandrange.com and email enquiries sent to Demand Range. It does not replace a service-specific privacy review, data-processing agreement, controller allocation, outreach notice, suppression process or contract. Those must be handled separately where required before live work.

3. Information we collect

Information you choose to provide

  • Name, work email, company, role and business contact details.
  • Enquiry text, product category, target market, facility context, commercial range and research challenge.
  • Follow-up correspondence and meeting notes you choose to share.

Technical website and email data

  • Basic browser/device information, requested pages, IP address, timestamps and hosting/security logs processed through website infrastructure.
  • Email delivery metadata such as sender, recipient, timestamps, message IDs and authentication results.
  • A light/dark theme preference, stored only in your own browser if you use the toggle and never sent to Demand Range.

We do not intentionally collect special-category data through this website, and the site is not aimed at children.

4. How and why we use it

  • Respond to enquiries and assess whether the service may be a fit.
  • Prepare non-sensitive follow-up materials and an initial working scope.
  • Manage business records, internal tasks and correspondence.
  • Operate, secure and troubleshoot the website and email route.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, tax, accounting or dispute-handling obligations where applicable.

5. Lawful bases

Depending on context, Demand Range may rely on steps before entering a contract and/or legitimate interests in responding to professional enquiries, managing B2B relationships and protecting the website. Legal obligation may apply to tax, accounting, regulatory or dispute records. Where consent is used, it may be withdrawn.

6. Sharing and service providers

Website and enquiry information is shared only where needed for the purposes above. Provider categories may include website hosting/source control, domain/DNS/security, email/productivity, internal operations and professional advisers or authorities where required. Demand Range does not sell personal data collected through this website.

7. International transfers

Some infrastructure or email providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or provider data-processing terms should apply. Any live client or campaign data requires a separate provider, role and transfer review.

8. Retention

Ordinary enquiry correspondence is reviewed and retained only while useful for the relationship or a legitimate business record, with a target review period of up to 24 months after the last meaningful interaction unless legal, tax, accounting, security or dispute needs require longer. Technical logs follow relevant infrastructure settings. The dr-theme colour preference stays in your browser until you change it or clear this site's browser storage; it is not sent to Demand Range.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and context, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. Contact partners@demandrange.com. Identity verification may be required.

10. Security and service data

Reasonable organisational and technical measures are used for the public website and enquiry route. Ordinary email is not appropriate for sensitive files, credentials or live client data. A suitable route, role allocation, sources, suppression handling, retention/deletion and provider review must be agreed before relevant live work.

Any future software or client-data processing would require tested customer isolation and controls preventing confidential client information from entering market outputs. This notice does not represent those controls as operating in a live software platform today.

11. Contact and complaints

Privacy contact: partners@demandrange.com. Demand Range has not appointed a formal Data Protection Officer for this website notice at this stage.

If you are in Ireland or the EU and are unhappy with how personal data is handled, you may have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission or your local supervisory authority. See dataprotection.ie.
