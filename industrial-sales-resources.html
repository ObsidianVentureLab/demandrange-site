Practical guides · Company-level industrial research

Industrial sales research guides and templates

Choose the job you need to do: find relevant companies, check the customer route, review a supplied list, keep accounts current or understand a research deliverable. These resources are written for industrial suppliers, with Ireland as the current research focus.

The worksheets are ungated educational starting points. Worked companies and decisions are explicitly fictional—not customer results or live prospects.

Check who the customer actually is

Use the supplier route before treating every company associated with a product as the same kind of prospect.

05 / review quality

Check a supplied prospect list

Use a worked exercise before accepting a batch or treating every spreadsheet row as a distinct company.

Work through the fictional industrial prospect-list audit, then use its batch, answer key and acceptance worksheets to challenge duplicates, holds and inclusion reasons.

The audit page keeps the fictional batch, answer key and blank templates together, so the teaching decisions can be read alongside their rules. The exercise is not an approval of a real list or its source-use rights.

06 / keep current

Keep an existing account list useful

Resolve a conflicting field without accepting the whole account — one fictional account, five evidence cards, four review events and a blank resolution worksheet.

When the targeting brief, company identity, operating site or commercial role changes, reopen the affected decision instead of silently overwriting the original evidence.

  • When to refresh an industrial prospect list — change triggers, a review workflow and fictional retain, hold, correct and remove decisions.
  • Blank review-event log (CSV) — record the change, evidence references, reason, owner and next check. Several events can refer to one company; they are not extra accounts.

Choose review timing around the actual use and available evidence. The resource does not assume a universal refresh interval or list-decay percentage.

09 / programme date downloads

Programme corrections and source dates

Manual research aids, not source validators or evidence of an executed correction. Read the date dictionary and attribution and completed correction note before using them.

  • Blank programme correction register (header-only CSV)
  • Fictional correction register with four dependent outputs (CSV)

The linked date guide contains separately attributed catalogue-date examples and a fictional date-conflict exercise. The two downloadable registers contain original templates and fictional teaching material only.

The blank register is header-only. Add one row per dependent output, preserve versions, and verify each actual correction. All four fictional readbacks are explicitly NOT EXECUTED; expected teaching outcomes are not proof that a document changed. Unknowns stay unknown; analyst withdrawal is not an issuer correction.

10 / Next step

Review the industrial research service for the make-versus-buy comparison, evidence boundaries and scoping questions. When the brief is clear, request a written scope. Counts, fields, timing and commercial terms remain subject to agreement.
