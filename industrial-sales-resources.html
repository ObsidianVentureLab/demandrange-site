Practical guides · Company-level industrial research

Industrial sales research guides and templates

Choose the job you need to do: find relevant companies, check the customer route, review a supplied list, keep accounts current or understand a research deliverable. These resources help industrial suppliers research target EU markets, including Ireland. Country-specific guides keep their stated scope: an Irish source or example does not establish coverage elsewhere. For a commissioned brief, source access, language requirements and deliverables are assessed country by country before scope is agreed.

The worksheets are ungated educational starting points. Worked companies and decisions are explicitly fictional—not customer results or live prospects.

01 / choose a task

Start with the decision you need to make

Choose the starting point that matches what you already have. Aim for the first useful output below, then use the specialist guides and downloads further down this page when that task needs them.

I have a product range, but no agreed account brief.
Define which companies belong. First output: a written brief covering activity, customer role, territory, exclusions and evidence rules. Use the guide’s blank ICP worksheet before sourcing names.
I have a brief and need to assemble a manufacturing shortlist.
Build and review a candidate list. First output: separate include, hold and reject decisions, with company/site distinctions and a next check for each hold—not a raw row count.
I know my product, but not who buys or specifies it.
Choose the customer route for pump and flow-control research. First output: a supplier-fit brief that distinguishes equipment builders, operators and service routes, with unresolved responsibility questions. Use the OEM, distributor or MRO guide below for the relevant specialist check.
I have received a prospect list and need to challenge it.
Start the supplied-batch review. First output: a reconciliation of distinct companies, duplicates, holds and inclusion reasons. Work the fictional batch and answer key before applying the blank audit tools to a permitted real list.
I already use a list, but an underlying fact or rule has changed.
Reopen the affected account decision. First output: a review event stating what changed, the evidence, the retain/hold/correct/remove decision and the next check. This is not a new list or an automatic refresh schedule.
I need to understand the record I would commission.
Inspect the fictional account-research sample. First output: the fields, evidence distinctions and unknowns you want to discuss in a delivery specification. Then compare the service and written-scope questions below; the sample is not proof of a client result.

Advanced task: allocate the next research block

Already screened the accounts, but have more open questions than research time? Prioritise research actions across industrial accounts. First output: a bounded action queue with estimates, reasons for selection or deferral, and remaining evidence gaps. This allocates analyst work; it does not rank sales prospects or validate a buying-intent score. The worked queue comes before the blank CSV’s field instructions.

Not sure which tool is needed for an assignment? Review the company-level research service and scope. No worksheet itself confirms account acceptance, source-use permission or authority to contact a person.
