Using Companies House to research UK industrial accounts

By Demand Range research team · Last reviewed 10 November 2026

To find UK industrial companies on Companies House, open advanced search, enter a SIC code, set the status to Active and download the results as a CSV. Then open each company's filings to check its accounts type and charges. Companies House shows the legal company, not its plants, equipment or buyers.

The manufacturing code list and any UK company counts sit on our UK SIC codes for manufacturing page. How this search fits a full UK account brief, including site confirmation, is in how to build a list of UK manufacturers. The worked example below is fictional.

01 / key facts

Key facts

  • Free to search: all public data Companies House holds on companies is available free of charge, and you do not need to register to view it (guidance last updated 9 June 2026).[5]
  • One to four codes: at least one SIC code, and up to four.[9]
  • Worked search, 10 November 2026: SIC 28131 (Manufacture of pumps), status Active, showed 469 results.[2][3]
  • Download limit: results are downloaded as a CSV file; only the first 5,000 can be downloaded.[3]
  • Registered office: a physical UK address, which may be a service provider's (for example an accountant or solicitor) and is published on the register.[6]
  • Size tests (any two): small — turnover £15 million or less, balance sheet £7.5 million or less, 50 employees or less; micro-entity — turnover £1 million or less, balance sheet £500,000 or less, 10 employees or less.[7]
02 / step 1 open advanced search and filter by sic code

Step 1: open advanced search and filter by SIC code

Fill in one or more fields: company name, registered office address, incorporation date, company status, nature of business (SIC code), company type, company subtype and dissolved date.[1] Nature of business takes a SIC code (example 01120) from the condensed SIC 2007 list, a condensed version of the full Office of National Statistics list; filings must use those codes or may be rejected.[1][2] Enter a five-digit code, for example 28131, "Manufacture of pumps".[2] Set status to Active (the form also lists Dissolved, Open, Closed, Converted closed, Removed, Receivership, Liquidation, Administration, Insolvency proceedings and Voluntary arrangement).[1] Dissolved-date results only cover companies dissolved since 1 January 2010.[1] The 10 November 2026 search showed 469 results, and some rows listed more than one of up to four codes.[3][9]

03 / step 2 export the results

Step 2: export the results

Results are downloaded as a CSV file, and only the first 5,000 results can be downloaded.[3] If a code returns more, narrow the search by incorporation date or registered office address and download each slice.[1][3] For a pull across many codes, or over the 5,000 cap, see UK SIC codes for manufacturing. Note the search date.

04 / step 3 read the filings

Step 3: read the filings

Companies House says the service is not intended to be comprehensive.[5]

  • Accounts. Filings can show whether the company prepared full, small, micro-entity or dormant accounts. Small companies may send abridged accounts if all members agree, so less information is publicly available.[7]
  • Charges. Mortgage charge data is part of the free company information.[4][5] A recent charge can point to new financing, not to new equipment.
  • Confirmation statements. Every company must file one at least once every year, confirming the information held is up to date; it can record a SIC code change.[8]
05 / what companies house shows and what it does not

What Companies House shows, and what it does not

Companies House shows Companies House does not show
Registered office address[4][5][6] Trading sites or plant addresses
SIC codes filed by the company[2][5][9] The processes run at each site
Current and resigned officers[4][5] Engineering, maintenance or purchasing contacts
Filed accounts and accounts type[5][7] Equipment installed or planned
Charges, previous names and insolvency information[4][5] Projects, tenders or budgets
06 / why a registered office is not a plant

Why a registered office is not a plant

The registered office must be a physical UK address, in the same country the company is registered in, and appropriate for official post. GOV.UK says it may be a service provider's address, for example an accountant or solicitor.[6] Confirm the operating site from a second source; see how to build a list of UK manufacturers.

07 / a worked example fictional

A worked example (fictional)

This example is fictional. "Hartwell Flow Systems Limited" appears in a search for SIC 28131 with status Active. Its registered office is a service provider's address. Its filings show small company accounts, a charge registered eight months ago, and SIC 28131 and 33120 (Repair of machinery).[2] Record a possible fit subject to site confirmation, a small-company size signal, and the company name and number, not officer names.

08 / turning a search into an account list

Turning a search into an account list

  1. Run one advanced search per SIC code in the brief, status Active.[1]
  2. Merge exports and remove duplicates by company number.
  3. Review dormant filings and holding companies; record a hold or reject reason.
  4. Confirm the operating site and activity using how to build a list of UK manufacturers.

09 / frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Companies House advanced search free?
Yes. Companies House makes the public data it holds on companies available free of charge, and you do not need to register to view it. You can run an advanced search, download a CSV, and read filings without paying. Free email alerts are available if you sign in after registering.
How many results can I download from advanced search?
Only the first 5,000 results of a search can be downloaded, as a CSV file. If a search returns more than 5,000 companies, narrow it by incorporation date or registered office address and download each slice. Keep the export and note the search date.
Does an Active search for one SIC code list every company in that activity?
No. An Active search on one SIC code is only a dated snapshot of companies that have filed that code. A company may file up to four codes, and some relevant companies may use a neighbouring or outdated code. Use the export as a candidate pool, then confirm the activity from other evidence.
Can I tell a company's size from Companies House?
Partly. Accounts filings can show whether the company filed as small, micro-entity or dormant. GOV.UK sets size tests as any two of the published turnover, balance-sheet and employee limits. Small companies may send abridged accounts if all members agree, so less information is public.
Should I store directors' names from the register in a prospect list?
Only if you have a clear lawful reason. Companies House publishes current and resigned officers as free public data, but this research is about the company or site, not the people. The company name and number usually identify the legal unit. Named contacts are a separate scope.
Does a SIC code prove that a company manufactures that product?
No. A SIC code is the nature of business the company files, taken from the condensed list. It can be generic, out of date, or only one of up to four activities. Use the code to build a candidate pool, then confirm the activity and site from other evidence.
