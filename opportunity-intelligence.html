What you receive

Each engagement is scoped to a defined category, territory and review period. Outputs are delivered as working research, not as a self-serve or real-time software product.

Opportunity register

  • Facility and company mapping
  • Observed event and evidence trail
  • Category-fit analysis
  • Buying-window hypothesis

Route-to-market view

  • Buying-role map
  • Business contact routes where appropriate
  • Known unknowns and confidence
  • Recommended action and review date
Updates by agreement: change reviews can be included at a contracted cadence. Demand Range does not currently represent the service as a continuous alerting platform.

A company name is not an opportunity.

A credible Demand Range record identifies a facility or event, shows its evidence, explains the plausible link to your offering, makes uncertainty visible and recommends a next step.

Observed

The dated source record: what was published, by whom and when.

Inferred

The analyst's commercial interpretation, explicitly separated from fact.

Unknown

What still needs qualification before the thesis should drive action.

Where the service fits best

  • Complex products with commercially meaningful deal values.
  • Consultative, technical or specification-led sales.
  • Identifiable plants, utilities, projects or operational assets.
  • A sales team able to qualify and follow up.
  • Value in engaging before formal procurement.

Where it is less suitable

  • High-volume commodity sales with little need for account context.
  • A request for anonymous web-intent data or guaranteed appointments.
  • A category with no defined facility, project or change hypothesis.

Scope before volume.

The initial sprint establishes the source playbook, opportunity definition and acceptance criteria before wider coverage. The work remains analyst-led; any future software layer will be shaped by paid use and evidence, not presented as already operating.
