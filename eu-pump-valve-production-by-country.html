Where the EU makes pumps and valves, country by country

By Demand Range research team · Last reviewed 24 November 2026

If you sell into pump and valve makers, or sell their products on, start from where the production actually is. Eurostat's Prodcom statistics give sold production value by product and country, with exports and imports matched to the same product headings.

Demand Range researches industrial accounts in Ireland, the UK and the EU. This page owns one thing: the country picture for two classes, 28.13 (other pumps and compressors, 42 Prodcom headings) and 28.14 (other taps and valves, 22 headings), for 2024 and 2025. For how Prodcom works in general, see our Prodcom guide. For how to turn the figures into an account list, see pump and flow-control prospecting.

01 / key facts

Key facts

Eurostat Prodcom DS-059367 for 2025 and DS-059358 for 2024; extracted 24 November 2026.

  • EU-27 sold production of pumps and compressors was about €33.2bn in 2025, up from about €31.9bn in 2024 (about +3.8%). Taps and valves were about €27.7bn in both years (about −0.2%). These are our sums of Eurostat's EU-27 heading values, many of which Eurostat rounds or estimates.[1][2][3]
  • Germany publishes the most pump and compressor production, at least €12.9bn in 2025, then Italy at €5.3bn or more. Italy publishes the highest valve production, at least €9.5bn, ahead of Germany at €7.5bn or more. Both are by our count of published headings only.[1]
  • Eurostat's EU-27 trade figures cover only trade with countries outside the EU. Country trade figures include trade with other member states, so the two never add up.[3]
  • Ireland had 40 of the 64 pump and valve production headings marked confidential in 2025, the most of any EU country by our count. By our sums, it imported €398.9m of pump and compressor products and €345.8m of taps and valves.[1]

Confidentiality moved a lot between the two years. Spain had 19 confidential headings in 2024 and none in 2025, while Ireland went from 20 to 40, by our count. Across the 27 member states the total barely moved: 409 of 1,728 heading cells in 2024 and 399 in 2025.[1][2]

05 / why eu trade totals are extra eu only

Why EU trade totals are extra-EU only

Eurostat's Prodcom metadata is explicit: "For EU totals, where production and trade data for all EU countries is aggregated, only trade with countries outside the EU is included."[3]

A country's export figure includes its sales to other EU countries. So adding up country exports double-counts trade inside the EU, and it is not comparable with the EU-27 row:[1][3]

  • Pumps and compressors, 2025: the 27 member states' exports sum to €52.2bn by our count, but the EU-27 heading values sum to €24.6bn of extra-EU exports.
  • Taps and valves, 2025: member-state exports sum to €34.8bn by our count, against €18.0bn of extra-EU exports by our sum of the EU-27 heading values.

On the extra-EU measure, the EU is a net exporter of both classes: about €12.9bn for pumps and compressors and about €8.0bn for taps and valves in 2025, by our count.[1]

Two more cautions. First, some country trade does not reflect manufacturing. The Netherlands and Belgium show large pump exports (€3.8bn and €3.4bn in 2025, by our sums) but publish only 2 and 1 of 42 pump production headings, with 21 and 17 confidential. Their production cannot be read from this data, and our inference is that their trade likely includes distribution and transit flows. Second, Prodcom matches trade to Prodcom headings, which differ from customs tariff headings, so do not mix these figures with customs-based trade tables.[1][4]

06 / ireland imports exports and confidential cells

Ireland: imports, exports and confidential cells

Ireland is a net importer of both classes. In 2025 it exported €223.7m of pump and compressor products and imported €398.9m. For taps and valves, exports were €130.3m and imports €345.8m. All four figures are our sums of the headings in each class, and they include trade with other EU countries.[1]

Source: Eurostat Prodcom DS-059358 (2024) and DS-059367 (2025), reporter Ireland, EXPVAL and IMPVAL, all 64 headings. Extracted 24 November 2026.[1][2]

Irish production is mostly hidden. In 2025, 40 of Ireland's 64 headings were confidential, the highest count in the EU, and 15 were published, by our count. The published headings add up to at least €75.3m for pumps and compressors and €19.1m for taps and valves. The largest single published heading was parts of pumps for liquids (28133100), at €44.4m.[1]

Do not compare Irish production across the two years. In the 2024 dataset, 20 Irish headings were confidential and the other 44 show zero with no flag, by our count; none shows a positive value. Those zeros do not prove there was no production: Ireland exported €216.9m of pump and compressor goods under the same headings that year, by our sum.[2]

Trade data alone can mislead. For safety or relief valves, Ireland's published 2025 production was €7.9m but its exports were €17.2m, more than twice as much, which points to re-exports or distribution. Where production is published, apparent consumption (production plus imports minus exports) can be worked out: for parts of pumps for liquids it was about €98.1m in 2025, by our count. For the 40 confidential headings it cannot, so there is no official total for the Irish pump and valve market.[1]

The United Kingdom is not in either dataset. For UK figures, see the Office for National Statistics release "UK manufacturers' sales by product (PRODCOM)".[5]

07 / frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Which EU country makes the most pumps?
Germany publishes the most. Its published 2025 sold production of pumps and compressors was at least €12.9bn, by our count, ahead of Italy at €5.3bn or more. The EU-27 total was about €33.2bn, by our sum. Figures sum published Prodcom headings only; confidential headings are left out, so country figures are minimums.[1]
Which EU country makes the most valves?
Italy publishes the highest valve production value: at least €9.5bn in 2025, by our count, ahead of Germany at €7.5bn or more. Germany keeps 4 of its 22 valve headings confidential, so the true order is not certain. EU-27 taps and valves production was about €27.7bn in 2025, by our sum.[1]
Did EU pump and valve production grow in 2025?
Pumps and compressors grew modestly: EU-27 sold production rose from about €31.9bn in 2024 to about €33.2bn in 2025, about +3.8% in current euro. Taps and valves were flat at about €27.7bn. These are our sums of Eurostat's EU-27 heading values, many of them rounded or estimated.[1][2]
Why can't I add up country exports to get EU exports?
Because the measures differ. Eurostat's EU-27 totals include only trade with countries outside the EU, while each country's exports include sales to other member states. In 2025, member-state pump and compressor exports summed to €52.2bn by our count, against €24.6bn of extra-EU exports by our sum of the EU-27 heading values.[1][3]
How big is the Irish pump and valve market?
There is no official figure. In 2025, 40 of Ireland's 64 pump and valve production headings were confidential, the most in the EU by our count. Ireland imported €398.9m of pumps and compressors and €345.8m of taps and valves, and exported €223.7m and €130.3m, by our sums. Imports alone are not market size.[1]
Does the data include the UK?
No. Eurostat's Prodcom datasets for 2024 and 2025 cover EU member states and some other European countries, but not the United Kingdom. UK figures are in the Office for National Statistics release "UK manufacturers' sales by product (PRODCOM)". Check its product codes and latest edition before comparing it with the EU tables.[5]
