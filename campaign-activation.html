Activation principles

Low volume. High relevance. Human review.

Activation is a scoped service, not an automated campaign engine. Channel, audience, claims and suppression requirements are agreed before outreach.

01

Specific reason

Every first touch is tied to an approved opportunity thesis.

02

Transparent claim

03

Human gate

Contacts and messaging are reviewed before activation.

04

Context handoff

Boundaries

What we do not do.

Trust is more valuable than a short-term volume target.

  • Mass generic email blasts
  • Purchased-list dumping
  • Silent phone-number probe calls
  • False claims of buyer intent
  • Guaranteed appointments
  • Undisclosed outreach to suppressed contacts
  • Activation without a human review gate
  • Sending from the public website
Operational boundary: this website has no transmitting form, campaign tooling, analytics or tracking. Any outreach service requires separate written scope, data/compliance review and client approval.
