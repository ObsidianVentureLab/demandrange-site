The sales problem

A long account list does not tell you why now.

Industrial sales teams need to connect plant-level change, commercial relevance and a credible route into the conversation.

01
Which plants are changing?

Expansion, upgrades, compliance work and operational investment happen at facilities—not abstract accounts.

02
What could the event create?

A signal matters only when it can be linked carefully to your category and commercial threshold.

03
When should you engage?

Too early lacks context. Too late leaves the specification and route to market already set.

04
Who shapes the decision?

Operators, engineers, contractors, integrators and procurement may each influence the outcome.

Scattered evidence

The clues rarely arrive in one place.

Planning records, licences, procurement notices, company announcements, contractor activity, hiring and local market context each reveal part of the picture.

Demand Range is an analyst-led service that connects approved sources into a practical, challengeable sales view. It is not a live software feed and does not claim access to private buyer intent.

How it works

From market definition to a sales-ready view.

Human judgement stays in the loop from source selection through activation.

Define the market

Agree product categories, facility types, territory, value threshold and covered accounts.

Detect & qualify

Review approved sources on the agreed cadence, resolve signals to facilities and retrieve evidence.

Map the route in

Assess relevance and identify roles, contractors and advisers likely to shape the purchase.

Activate & learn

Prepare approved outreach. When the client shares outcomes, that feedback can improve later analysis.

Starting engagement

A defined-scope Opportunity Intelligence Sprint.

A typical starting scope covers one product family, territory and priority market over five weeks. Final timing and deliverables are agreed in writing.

01

Market thesis

Category, facilities, trigger events, evidence sources, exclusions and acceptance criteria.

02

Opportunity register

Prioritised records with five deeper briefs and buying-role maps for agreed priority accounts.

03

90-day action view

Contact and activation recommendations, a working session and an account sequence.

Commercial scope: any engagement requires an approved proposal and separately agreed written terms.
