Overflow fulfilment gets risky when quality control is thin.

Vendors need extra capacity for tight account, title, geo, and client-template specs — but weak external fulfilment creates rejection risk, operational drag, and uncomfortable questions about where each record came from.

Hard-to-fill specs

Tight geos, senior titles, account lists, niche industries, and short delivery windows need controlled fulfilment, not broad scraping.

Messy lead data

Wrong titles, stale roles, generic inboxes, duplicate records, and weak verification slow campaign teams down.

Weak proof trails

Vendors need source, date, verification, asset, suppression, QA, and replacement evidence that can survive review.

A controlled fulfilment layer behind your demand programmes.

Demand Range is designed to support vendor delivery teams, not replace their platform, network, or client relationship. The motion is simple: review the spec, confirm what is allowed, deliver clean batches, and close the rejection loop.

AI-assisted lead QA. Human-reviewed delivery.

The AI angle is practical: catch questionable records before a vendor has to reject them. AI helps triage risk; humans keep the quality gate.

AI helps flag

  • Title/function mismatch risk.
  • Account, geo, industry, and company-size mismatch risk.
  • Missing source/proof fields.
  • Duplicate or stale-record risk.
  • Template-formatting gaps.

Humans decide

  • Whether a low-confidence record passes, fails, or needs review.
  • Whether legal/source/channel assumptions are clear enough to proceed.
  • Whether a batch is ready for vendor/client delivery.
  • How rejection patterns change the QA rules.

Where Demand Range fits

Content syndication, CPL/MQL programmes, asset-led outreach, strict campaign briefs, and vendors that want cleaner delivery without adding permanent fulfilment headcount.

Where we should say no

Guaranteed appointments, BANT-only telemarketing, low-quality scraping, unclear source permissions, unsupported regions/channels, or campaigns without suppression/legal-review clarity.
